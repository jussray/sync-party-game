## The gain mechanics these items must carry

1. **B3-04 — COMMENT mechanic (Mechanic Rule).** The post does not end on an open-ended question — it asks for one *specific small reply* with a promised payoff. Per the B3-04 spec, the prompt is: "What's the question your group chat would fail instantly?" — one question, answerable in a line, no personal details; the strongest replies get featured or run in the next post. Never "what do you think?", never "comment YES," never engagement-for-access.
2. **B3-05 — SEND mechanic (Mechanic Rule).** The caption names the exact person to send it to — your person, the one who swears the two of you answer everything the same — a specific person in a specific situation, never "share this," never "tag someone." The mismatch moment in the round is the sendable beat.
3. **Follow hook (both items).** Each caption names the item's place in the Batch #3 season (B3-04 / B3-05 of Batch #3) and what drops next, so following has a concrete reason attached to a concrete next thing. "Follow for more" is banned as filler.
4. **Door line (both items).** One caption line points at the live site as the door: https://sync.jussco.company
