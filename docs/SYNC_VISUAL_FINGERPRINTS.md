## Asset-level continuity

`visual/project.asset-manifest.json` is the machine-readable asset continuity record for these surfaces. The manifest and this fingerprint document must agree, but they prove different things:

- this document describes the intended visual and interaction identity;
- the manifest identifies the canonical source visuals, current implementation assets, provenance, prohibited substitutions, runtime references, known regressions, and rollback truth.

A matching palette or CSS treatment is not proof that a SYNC surface preserved its graphics. Founder-approved source visuals outrank prose descriptions and substitute implementation assets for graphic-fidelity decisions.

Until the manifest says otherwise with fresh source-vs-render evidence:

- Live Site graphic continuity is `REGRESSION`;
- repo-backed ChatGPT SYNC graphic continuity is `PARTIAL`;
- Lovable graphic continuity remains external-provider scoped in this repository.

Do not promote `public/party-art.png` or `public/chat/ai-host.svg` to canonical proof merely because those files exist or render. They are current implementation assets whose relationship to the approved source visuals must remain truthfully classified.

## CHATGPT_SYNC_VISUAL_V1

**Purpose:** the Chat Edition should feel like an AI-hosted social prediction experience, not a reskin of the arena.

**Visual fingerprint**
- cobalt / midnight-blue world with cyan electric edges and selective pink-violet accents
- friendly luminous AI host / robot presence near the top of the experience
- clean futuristic panels, speech bubbles, prediction bars, chat-like annotations
- handwritten side notes: “same prompt, different minds” and “can you predict the room?”
- people remain visible and social, but the AI host is the presentation anchor
- motion: scanner sweeps, chat-bubble drift, prediction-bar fills, soft robot glow, restrained neon pulses

**Experience fingerprint**
- Chat Edition adds a **surface-only prediction lens** before the player submits their authoritative SYNC answer
- first verb: predict what the room will choose
- second verb: submit your own answer to the shared SYNC room
- after reveal, compare the local prediction with the authoritative room distribution
- prediction state is local/presentation-only and must never mutate shared game authority, scores, room codes, tokens, or private identifiers

**Anti-drift**
- do not copy the warm Lovable lounge palette
- do not copy the live arena’s crowd / metallic headline / pressure-show grammar
- do not present local prediction as authoritative server state

**Anti-drift**

## LOVABLE_SYNC_VISUAL_V1

Lovable is maintained in its separate Lovable project. The approved fingerprint is warm human chemistry: deep plum/navy room, coral/pink/lavender/cyan/amber light, oversized bubbly LOVABLE SYNC lettering, real friend-group hero, hearts/doodles, tactile cards, and social-affinity reveals.

## Trilogy rule

The three experiences may share the SYNC name and social DNA, but their visual and interaction fingerprints must remain legibly distinct at a glance:

- **ChatGPT SYNC:** predict the room, AI-hosted cobalt future
- **Lovable SYNC:** match the vibe, warm human chemistry
- **Live Site SYNC:** survive the pressure, arena spectacle

A future change that collapses two surfaces into the same palette, hero composition, primary verb, reveal grammar, canonical asset family, character/subject treatment, or scene-depth grammar is a regression unless the founder explicitly approves the convergence.
