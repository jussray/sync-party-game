GOOD TO KNOW

SYNC Party questions, answered.

What is SYNC Party?

SYNC Party is a real-time multiplayer party game by Juss & Co. Everyone in the room answers the same prompt privately, beats the clock, and then all picks are revealed together. The goal is to read the room: pick what everyone else picks — or, in some modes, deliberately don't.

How many players can join a SYNC Party game?

A SYNC Party room holds 2–8 players. One player creates the room and shares its five-character code; everyone else joins from their own phone or laptop.

Do I need an account or an app to play SYNC Party?

No. SYNC Party needs no account, no login, and no app install. It plays right in the web browser on phones and laptops — just open the site, create or join a room, and pick a nickname.

How does a SYNC Party game work?

A game lasts 5 rounds. Each round shows one prompt; every player makes a private pick within 15 seconds, picks lock, and then the room reveals together and sees how in sync it was. Round winners score 3 points, and the highest score wins the game.

What are the five SYNC Party modes?

Classic Sync — pick the same as the room. Twin — match exactly one other player. Odd One Out — be the only one to pick it. Reverse — the smallest group wins. Perfect Sync — everyone must match. A different mode runs each round.

Is SYNC Party free to play?

Yes. SYNC Party is free to play in the browser, with no account and no install.
